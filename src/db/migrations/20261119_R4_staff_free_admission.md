# R4 staff-free admission — prepared, not deployed

Apply only after explicit approval, on the reviewed R2 CARD/avatar and R3 catalog → checkin → bonus → override stack, after `20261119_R4_staff_free_foundation.sql`. The new migration checks both installed post-override admission bodies and invoker settings before a guarded rewrite. R6/R7 are not supported. The stock R6 textual attachment is deliberately rejected after R4 so R7 cannot silently become the assisted admission path on this stack. Do not force either migration through; design and test a staff-aware R6/R7 integration first.

## Admission contract

- Kiosk and assisted frontdesk use the existing member lock and admission clock. R2 CARD/BLU routing, staff session and station checks, avatar response, replay, bounce, and entry-only behavior remain in place. With no candidate covering the checked instant, the existing October/R3 paid slot and welcome gates are unchanged. R4 does not make `members.expire_date` a staff-inclusive expiry or change any membership/profile/reporting reader.
- A staff candidate is eligible only for an `ACTIVE` `MEMBER` with the bound code, a single R3-proven PAID predecessor, matched package ID and expiry, and no PENDING order. Reuse the R3 proof at the recorded sale instant, not at the scan instant after the predecessor expires. The staff term is `[starts_at, ends_at)`; the immutable sale's Bangkok slot is opening-inclusive and closing-exclusive. There is no October closing exception or R3 override on a staff term. An existing override may still apply to the predecessor before `starts_at`.
- An active candidate prevents fallback to a welcome coupon when off-slot or when its provenance is unsafe (e.g. overlapping candidates, expiry drift, PENDING with no proven successor). Once the staff interval ends the ordinary welcome rules apply again. A later online PAID can take precedence **only** if it is the order selected by the existing PAID ordering, was created after the sale, is uniquely latest by confirmation, has the matching ledger/transfer provenance, valid slot, and a live expiry matching the member aggregate. It must still pass the ordinary online slot gate; it cannot borrow the staff slot or revive the staff term when ineligible. If those conditions are ambiguous during the staff interval, deny rather than choose a slot or redeem a coupon. Online settlement and its expiry/benefit calculation are not changed; the overlapping staff time is not banked, extended, refunded, or automatically reconciled. Outside the staff interval the legacy behavior is intentionally not redesigned.
- The private reader distinguishes `STAFF_FREE`, proven superseding `ONLINE_PAID`, and `BLOCKED`; no row means unchanged legacy admission. A successful staff check-in records `STAFF_FREE_ENTRY term=STAFF_FREE:<sale_id>` in the existing check-in note, with zero points. `PAID_ENTRY` remains the existing **response code for admission by a package** so current UI labels work; it is neither a source discriminator nor a paid order. No synthetic PAID, slip, ledger entry, coupon issuance/redemption, or finance write is performed for a staff admission. No existing check-in or cached response is rewritten.

## Before enabling a writer

1. Do not install the test prototype on a real DB. A production writer must prove live `NORMAL` ADMIN/OWNER, revalidate after waits, bind and dedupe actor/request + canonical inputs, use DB time, lock the member `FOR UPDATE` before reading eligibility (same serialization as online order creation/settlement and admission), and validate a locked current catalog snapshot. It must enforce exactly one standalone predecessor, no PENDING or overlapping staff terms, same SKU, contiguous future start, zero fee, no bonus days/points or friend coupons, and atomic audit/finance provenance. No backdate, chaining across multiple PAIDs, stacking, status/aggregate rewrite or manual insertion. The reader trusts these write-time actor/catalog facts: they cannot be reconstructed from a mutable catalog or live session at scan time.
2. Agree with operations on the later-online conflict/precedence behavior above and verify end-to-end expiry/status compatibility. In particular, a job that sets `EXPIRED` from the legacy expiry alone would block staff admission. Other consumers still see the old expiry and must not be advertised as staff-aware. Provide a separate OWNER finance read/export integration for the 0-baht `finance_source` and verify ADMIN cannot read financial history. No issuance or refund workflow is included here.
3. Validate exact production definitions, RLS/default ACLs and backend ownership/privileges before applying. The new helper is invoker-only and private; live scans run at `READ COMMITTED`. The trusted DB principal needs private schema usage, helper execute and read access to `free_sales`, plus its existing admission/R3 permissions. This migration gives no grants and does not expose the private reader as an app RPC. Do not grant private read/write access to customer or staff login roles. Rehearse backup, restoration, live traffic locking and physical scan acceptance separately.

## Validation and rollback

Run the two scratch native PostgreSQL checks (no application env or production connection):

```sh
python -B scripts/r4-staff-free-admission-postgres-test.py --bin-dir .local-db/portable-postgres-uat/package/native/bin --psql .local-db/portable-postgres-uat/psql.exe
python -B scripts/r4-staff-free-postgres-test.py --bin-dir .local-db/portable-postgres-uat/package/native/bin --psql .local-db/portable-postgres-uat/psql.exe
```

The admission harness uses synthetic PostgreSQL 18.4 data with real migration, payment/finalizer, check-in and R2 wrapper SQL. Only the admission clock is pinned for boundary scans. It checks source drift rollback, the unsupported R6/R7 paths, October/R3 time boundaries, staff expiry and slot boundaries, audit attribution, override separation, replay/bounce, late-write rollback, conflicts, and a second connection holding a later settlement while a scan starts. It is not verification against actual production data, roles, browser/USB, scheduler or finance export.

A failed migration rolls back its DDL. Before any approved application capture both installed definitions **and exact `pg_proc.prosrc`**, ACLs, and owners. With traffic and any future writer paused and impact reviewed, undo by transactionally restoring the two captured R3 definitions in place (not rerunning an old migration). Retain `free_sales`, `finance_source`, prior check-ins/notes, and cached requests. New admissions would no longer honor staff terms; existing replies must not be erased or recomputed. Do not use rollback as silent revocation after issuance. The native harness exercises restoring the original function bodies and retaining sale evidence.
