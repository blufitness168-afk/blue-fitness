## Behavior

- The R4 reader still decides whether an explicit staff sale is active, blocked, cancelled or superseded. No SALE/PAID rows or entitlement intervals are made up. The legacy online admission fallback remains unchanged, but it is not proof for a new supplement. For online supplements, require the R3 standalone proof and the cancellation-aware selected order. For a staff supplement, require the exact active `STAFF_FREE` or `STAFF_TRANSFER` sale. A scheduled sale is not active; no binding rolls forward from its online predecessor. Amounts, payment evidence, points, aggregate expiry, cancellation compensation and existing business rows are not rewritten.
- Staff must explicitly grant a coupon; a member must explicitly activate it. The immutable grant binds exactly one order or sale, its snapshot and expiry. Recheck on activation and admission under the member lock. The existing Bangkok window, quota, replay and earliest-expiry rules apply. A used online supplement blocks cancellation as a consumed benefit even without a visit. An unused grant does not block an otherwise eligible cancellation and cannot be activated afterwards.
- Kiosk and frontdesk persist the exact source, package and used slot at admission: welcome, online base/override, or staff term, with a supplement only when it actually admitted the member. Staff snapshots store `staffSaleId` and no `orderId`. Both write the check-in and one shared open visit atomically, including legacy entry RPC callers making a **new** request. Prior ENTRY_ONLY receipts are replayed as recorded; no old row is assigned an OUT or synthesized visit. OUT closes an existing visit without granting entry or awarding points.
- Manual visits remain observed history only: OWNER can record a pair with an explicitly corroborated online order, unknown package, or (after the manual-staff extension) a provable staff sale at the recorded IN time. Manual history never changes entitlements. Ambiguous online history and applying an online override to a staff sale are not supported.

Only the scratch test advances the business clock to exercise scheduled-to-active transitions. The standalone kiosk/manual and R4 online cancellation suites and 25 focused Node tests also passed.
